LIMITATIONS
These are theoretical BER estimates derived from the simulated link SNR values.
They do not establish real-world satellite-link performance.
The reported Random Forest selection accuracy was evaluated on synthetic,
rule-generated scenarios and should not be treated as independent test accuracy.
