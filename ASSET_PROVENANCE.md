## Earth textures

The frontend currently uses these five Earth texture files:

- `earth_atmos_2048.jpg`
- `earth_clouds_1024.png`
- `earth_lights_2048.png`
- `earth_normal_2048.jpg`
- `earth_specular_2048.jpg`

They are present under `public/textures/` and are copied into
`frontend/public/textures/` (and, after a build, `frontend/dist/textures/`).
These files are retained for the existing dashboard and are not being removed
or altered by this remediation.

The original source, creator, and applicable license or permission for each
of the five Earth textures have not yet been confirmed. The five Earth
textures require source/license confirmation before redistribution, including
in packaged releases or other repositories. Do not infer or claim that the MIT
license covers them. Once confirmed, record the source and exact license or
permission here and preserve any required attribution or notice.
